About

Jonah Dragan is an author, early childhood educator and professional storyteller who has been writing for most of his life. His stories are full of fantastical settings and unusual characters with relatable, human feelings. To his preschool students, he is Mr. Dragan.

Jonah is a member of the Society of Children’s Book Writers and Illustrators and sits on the Bank Street Children’s Book Committee. He lives in Brooklyn with his husband and a great many books.

The project

1000 Picture Books

From July 2020 to April 2023, Jonah reviewed one picture book a day on Instagram: a thousand books in a thousand days. The full archive lives here, and you can search it by title, author or illustrator.
